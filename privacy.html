Privacy Policy.

Effective Date: August 2026

At First In A Lifetime, accessible from https://firstinalifetime.com, your privacy is respected just as much as the open road. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by First In A Lifetime and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our site—whether by signing up for our email waitlist, subscribing to updates, or dropping a line—we may collect basic personal information such as your email address and name. Additionally, we automatically collect standard analytical data via Google Analytics (gtag.js) to understand how visitors navigate our digital memoirs, recipes, and photography archives.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our website and physical art drops.
  • Send you periodic emails, journal notes, and launch notifications for limited-run product drops (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • Analyze site traffic and user behavior to improve our content layout, recipes, and storytelling experience.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, First In A Lifetime uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. This information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

4. Third-Party Privacy Policies

First In A Lifetime's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. We advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party services (such as Google Analytics or social media platforms linked on our site) for more detailed information.

5. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at firstinalifetime@gmail.com.
